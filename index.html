HOW I DESIGN

The thinking behind every screen.

I design for the real user, not the assumed one.
I make complex products feel obvious.
I start with the problem behind the brief.
I test in the environment the product will actually live in.

I’m Elizabeth
Akinsola, a
product designer based
in Lagos.

I design interfaces for AI products, SaaS platforms, and startup MVPs — from the first research conversation to a polished handoff teams can actually build.

I don’t start with decoration. I start with the person on the other side of the screen: what they need to do in the first 30 seconds, what might confuse them, and what would make the product feel obvious.

My work lives at the intersection of empathy and clarity. Across web, mobile, and brand, I aim for screens that look considered and still move a business forward.

I’m open to full-time roles, internships, and select freelance projects — on-site in Lagos or remote. If you’re building something and need a designer who takes ownership, let’s talk.
